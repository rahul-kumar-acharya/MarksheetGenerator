Frequently Asked Questions

How do I search and print my online marksheet?
Go to the Find Result page, select your state and school name, then enter your Grade, Section, Roll Number, and Date of Birth. Once submitted, click the "Download / Print PDF" button to print or save a official transcript copy.
Why does it say "Result not uploaded yet"?
This message appears if your class teacher has not uploaded the marks for the selected exam cycle (Half Yearly or Final), or if the school administration has not yet verified the grade records. Please contact your school administrator.
How do I register a new school on EduMarks?
School principals can register their institution by clicking Register School. Fill in the official school details, affiliation board, and upload the principal's signature and school logo. Once verified by the superadmin, full portal access is activated.
How are theory and practical marks validated?
When creating subjects, teachers define maximum theory (e.g. 70) and practical marks (e.g. 30). The system automatically enforces strict validation so entered marks can never exceed the designated max limits.
Are online marksheets digitally verified?
Yes! Every generated transcript includes official institutional credentials, board affiliation details, class teacher signatures, and principal seals hosted securely on encrypted cloud storage.
