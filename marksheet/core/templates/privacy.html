Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 4, 2026 • EduMarks Data Protection System

1. Overview & Data Protection Principles

EduMarks ("we", "our", or "the Platform") is committed to protecting the privacy of educational institutions, administrators, teachers, parents, and students. This Privacy Policy details how we collect, store, process, and safeguard personal and academic data.

2. Information We Collect

To provide secure academic record management and verified transcript generation, we collect:

  • Institutional Data: School Name, Board Affiliation, Affiliation Number, Official Email, Phone Number, State Region, Pincode, and Principal Signature/Seal.
  • Educator & Staff Data: Principal/Teacher Administrative Usernames, Passwords (hashed), and assigned Classrooms.
  • Student Academic Data: Student Full Name, Roll Number, Date of Birth, Class/Section, Subjects, Theory & Practical Exam Marks.

3. How We Use Your Information

We process collected information strictly for legitimate educational operations:

  • Generating verified student marksheets and digital academic transcripts.
  • Enabling authenticated access for school principals and teachers to manage grade rosters.
  • Verifying official school registrations through administrative state controls.
  • Ensuring automated error validation and performance metrics calculation.

4. Security & Encryption Standard

We implement industry-standard security protocols to protect academic data against unauthorized access, alteration, or disclosure:

  • Data Encryption: All sensitive credentials and database transactions run over SSL/TLS encrypted channels.
  • Media Protection: School documents and seals are managed via secure Cloudinary storage with restricted raw media controls.
  • Password Hashing: User account passwords are stored using salted PBKDF2/SHA256 password hash algorithms.

5. Data Sharing & Third Parties

EduMarks does never sell, rent, or trade student or institutional data to commercial third parties or advertisers. Student marksheets are accessible only through exact lookup inputs (State, School, Grade, Section, Roll Number, and Birth Date).

6. Data Retention & School Control

Institutions retain full ownership of their academic records. Verified school administrators can update student details, modify class lists, or request complete account termination by contacting EduMarks governance.
